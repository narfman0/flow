# Characters

## Main Characters

### Kai
Acrobatic, fast, can't sit still. Electric orange and deep shadow color palette. His existential dread: afraid nothing he does actually matters, so he never stops moving.

**Apparent enemy → ally**: A rival courier who keeps outrunning him and stealing his jobs. Turns out she's been secretly helping him by taking the jobs he'd have failed. Becomes his most chaotic friend.

---

### Sela
Sharp, adaptable, hard to read. Cool violet and sharp white color palette. Her arc is about identity — she's been someone different for every person in her life. First person she's been fully herself around is Kai. Love interest.

**Apparent enemy → ally**: A researcher who seems to be investigating and following her, taking notes. Actually writing a book about extraordinary people and finds her fascinating. Deeply awkward but earnest.

---

### Daven
Older, dry, seen too much. Olive and rust color palette. Has encountered strangeness before and has been quietly trying to understand it. His dread is backward-facing: made a choice once and has never been sure it was right.

**Apparent enemy → ally**: A former colleague he had a falling out with years ago who keeps showing up. They argue constantly and bicker like an old married couple. Completely loyal underneath it.

---

### Pell
Youngest, manic wonder, treats everything like a field trip. Acid green and sky blue color palette. Their apparent fearlessness is actually denial.

**Apparent enemy → ally**: A skeptic who shows up to debunk everything Pell is excited about. Gets proven wrong repeatedly. Slowly becomes a true believer and is insufferably enthusiastic about it.

---

## Relationship Notes

- Kai and Sela: the romantic core; earns resolution quietly, not declaratively
- All four apparent enemies eventually appear together in Act 3 when it matters
- Rival banter and quirky alliances are the emotional heart of the game
