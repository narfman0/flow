# Art Style

## Visual Identity

Inspired by the anime *Lazarus* (Watanabe, 2025): vibrant, saturated, high-contrast. Low-poly 3D using Synty Studios assets. Third-person platforming camera.

## Color Language

Each character and their associated world/level has a distinct palette. When characters share scenes, palettes blend.

| Character | Primary     | Secondary   |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Kai       | Electric orange | Deep shadow |
| Sela      | Cool violet | Sharp white |
| Daven     | Olive       | Rust        |
| Pell      | Acid green  | Sky blue    |

The Fracture level strips color entirely, then returns it all at once at the end.

## The Wall Effect

Appears 4–5 times total across the game. A **visual punctuation moment**: when a character has a major realization or something significant shifts narratively, the walls breathe inward for a few seconds, colors hyper-saturate, the world holds its breath — then releases.

Not horror. Not gameplay-blocking. A stylistic comma.

## The Fracture Level

One dedicated level (Act 3) where geometry fully breaks: portals, overlapping spaces, non-Euclidean platforming. Withheld until late so it lands with full impact.

## Asset Base

Synty Studios low-poly 3D packs. glTF export from asset server → Bevy asset loader.
